# K1 — Control state machines (task of igs-iec61850-mms)

## Prompt

Run task K1 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md` (dependency A4), in parallel with P2: the sans-I/O direct and select-before-operate state
machines with normal and enhanced security, with selection, timeout, ownership, origin, ctlNum, test and check (CTL-001,
CTL-002). Done when the tests cover every transition and refusal.

## Summary

- Core, `control`: the types (models, configuration, requests, verdicts, responses, actions) and the machine (`control::machine`).
  The four models follow 7-2 Figures 40, 42, 43 and 44; the refusals follow Table 108. D-020.
- The figures were read on the PDF (7-2 pages 157, 160, 163 and 165 of the raw numbering): the figures are not transcribed in the
  prepared text. The reading is the authorised one of CONTEXT §10.
- Ownership: the selecting client; other clients are refused. Sequence: origin, test flag and value are fixed by the first
  request and checked on the next ones.
- Application checks and commands are actions answered by events (CTL-003); the machine never blocks.
- Two corrections found by the tests: the deselect timer was disarmed for the direct models (which have none), and a failed check
  now ends the selection, as the figures show.

## Tests

- 31 deterministic tests (`tests/control.rs`): the direct normal model (success, failed operative and dynamic tests, timeout,
  behaviours `test/blocked` and `off`, a second operation, a cancel by the operating client and by another one); the SBO normal
  model (select, operate-once and operate-many, another client, no selection, refused selection, deselect timeout, cancel,
  association loss, the wrong model); the direct enhanced model (change terminated positively, change timeout, cancel refused
  during the change, mirrored command, association loss); the SBO enhanced model (selection with its value and its check,
  refused selection, inconsistent value); the sequence checks, the catalogue names of the causes, and the determinism.
- Gate: fmt, clippy with `-D warnings` on all targets and all features, the workspace tests (156 passed), `cargo deny`.

## Not done here

  (deferred outcomes and the client control API). TimeActivatedOperate is declared unsupported in K2.
