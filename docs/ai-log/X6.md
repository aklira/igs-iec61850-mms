## Prompt

Run task X6 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md` (production phase X): the time source written in the PIXIT, the PTP decision, and the leap-second
behaviour, tested (D-052 left the announced leap second out of the stamps).

## Summary

- The announced positive leap second (leap indicator 1): the stamp whose SecondSinceEpoch is a midnight is marked `LeapSecondsKnown` FALSE
  while the last accepted reply announces it (7-2 Annex F.3: the leap second 23:59:60 has the value of the midnight, and its flag is FALSE).
  A stamp a second later is TRUE. A reply without the announcement keeps the midnight TRUE. A deletion (leap indicator 2) has no value in
  7-2 and is not signalled (D-055). Tests in `igs-iec61850-mms-core/tests/sntp.rs` (three, at controlled time).
- The PIXIT (`docs/conformance/PIXIT.md`) states: the time source (the host clock with its declared quality, or the embedded SNTP client), the
  SNTP poll and validity, the accuracy rule, the leap second, and PTP not implemented.
