# B1 — BRCB state machine and history (task of igs-iec61850-mms)

## Prompt

Run task B1 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md` (depends on D3): a bounded event history, `EntryID`, purge, overflow, and resynchronisation after the loss of an
association, in the same process (BRCB-001 to 003). Done when the tests of disconnect, reconnect, resume and overflow are deterministic and pass.

## Summary

- `crates/igs-iec61850-mms-core/src/report/brcb.rs`: the buffered block as a sans-I/O machine, the same shape as the unbuffered one (D3): `handle(now,
  Event) -> Vec<Action>`. States `disabled`, `resync`, `enabled` (7-2 figure 26); a bounded history of entries; the open buffer-time window; the integrity
  period; `BufOvfl`; `SqNum` (16 bits).
- The reading was done from the prepared text of IEC 61850-7-2 (17.2.2 and 17.2.3, figure 26 from its page and prose). Several figures of that text are
  marked "missing, transcribe manually" (among them figures 28 to 30 and 32 to 34: the buffer time, the sequence number, the general interrogation and
  the overflow examples). Figure 26 (the state machine) is in its raw page text, with the arrows partly lost; the transitions come from that text and
  from the prose. Where the text is open the choice is recorded in D-037 (the NULL `EntryID` in `resync`, the rule for a second notification).
- Known gap, recorded in D-037: entries keep members and reasons, not values; the values are read at formatting time. B2 carries the value in the
  notification.
- `capabilities.tsv`: BRCB-001 is "implemented at the state machine (served and encoded in B2)" with its six tests; `docs/statements.md` regenerated.

## Tests

- `igs-iec61850-mms-core/tests/report_buffered.rs`: fifteen deterministic tests, with controlled time (`ms(...)`), covering the history bound, the loss
  flag (only for entries not yet sent), resynchronisation to an entry and to the oldest one, the refusals by state, the purge and the configuration
  refusals while enabled, the disconnect and resumption, the integrity period and its stale timers, the sequence number, and the last `EntryID`.
- Gate: `cargo fmt --all --check`, clippy with `-D warnings` on all targets and all features and without `scl`, the workspace tests with all features,
  `cargo deny check`, and `cargo xtask statements --check` (AT-14).
