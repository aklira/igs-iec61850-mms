# O3 — Setting groups (task of igs-iec61850-mms)

## Prompt

Run task O3 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md` (optional group, section O of the plan): the setting group control block (SGCB) and its services
(IEC 61850-8-1 §16; 7-2 §16).

## Summary of this session

- Read the SGCB model: 7-2 §16.2 (the attributes and their defaults) and §16.3 (the services and their conditions); 8-1 §16.1 (the mapping to
  an MMS named component of LLN0 with FC SP) and §16.2 (each service is a specialisation of an MMS read or write, so no new PDU).
- Core: `src/settings.rs`, the state machine `Sgcb` (the active group, the edited group, the holder of the editing, `CnfEdit`, `LActTm`,
  `ResvTms` from the holder) and its rules. Nine tests with controlled time (`tests/settings.rs`); they found one defect (the flag `CnfEdit`
  was cleared after it was set), fixed in the same change.
- Gate: fmt, clippy (`-D warnings`), 354 tests, `statements --check`.

## Served part (second session)

- The served SGCB: `src/setting_groups.rs` (the device's groups, the values of each group, the rules applied to the served values),
  wired into `src/model.rs` (the reads and writes by name, before the VMD, with the association that calls; the release on association loss).
- Tests: `tests/setting_groups.rs` (three, over MMS, with the fixture `tests/data/settings.cid`): the structure read, the holder's
  editing with the refusal of the other association, the confirmation into the group and the activation, the bounds of the group number,
  and the release of the editing when the holder's association is closed.
- Gate: fmt, clippy (`-D warnings`), 357 tests, `statements --check`.

## Not done

- GetNameList does not list the SGCB; it is answered by name only (a decision, D-050).
- A storage that takes time: the storage is complete at once (a local issue, 7-2 §16.3.5).
