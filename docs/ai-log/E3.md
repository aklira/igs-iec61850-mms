# E3 — Edition 1 interop (task of igs-iec61850-mms): blocked

## Status

Blocked. The bench has no Edition 1 peer, and the box of E3 stays unticked in `IMPLEMENTATION.md`.

## What was tried (black box only; AGENTS.md forbids reading the libiec61850 source)

- The namespace of the reference device: `LLN0$EX$NamPlt$ldNs` of `server_example_basic_io` reads as an empty string. The edition cannot be
  read from it.
- The behaviour of the reference control example (`server_example_control`, object `GGIO1$CO$SPCSO1` to `SPCSO6`): about forty sequences of
  direct operate, select, select-with-value, test mode, check, origin, control numbers, cancel and select timeout. Positive terminations were
  obtained on `SPCSO3` and `SPCSO4`. Every refusal came from the write, with no termination and no additional cause. No
  `CommandTermination` with `Inconsistent-parameters` (26 in the second edition, 27 in the first) or `Locked-by-other-client` was observed, so the
  edition cannot be told from the causes.

The probe is outside the repository (scratch directory of the session) and is not committed.

## What would unblock it

- A peer that declares the first edition in its namespace (`ldNs` containing `2003`), or that produces a negative termination whose cause the
  first edition reads differently. The bench is limited to the peer image of `bench/peer/Dockerfile`.
- Captures or access to Edition 1 field devices (plan, section 5). This is the owner's decision.
- To check the claim of F4 that the documented configuration of libiec61850 offers an Edition 1 server mode: the public documentation of the project
  (outside the source), not the source.
