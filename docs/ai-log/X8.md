## Summary

- Review of the panics (`docs/robustness.md`): no panic macro, `unwrap`, `expect` or `assert` in the library code of the IEC crates nor of the
  MMS crates, outside the tests. The workspace lints (`unwrap_used`, `expect_used`, `indexing_slicing`, `arithmetic_side_effects`) enforce it.
- Hostile peer (`tests/hostile_peer.rs`): six kinds of hostile frames on raw TCP; a well-behaved client is served after each. Pass.
- Soak (`tests/soak.rs`): 300 cycles by default, 5 000 cycles run once (`IGS_SOAK_CYCLES`), with orderly closes, aborts and drops. 0 failures.
- Fuzzing: two new targets, `sntp_reply` (the SNTP client state) and `file_store` (the file store of `igs-iso9506-mms`), and the five of V3, each
  run for 60 seconds on nightly. No crash, no sanitizer report, empty artifact directories.
- Contention (`examples/contention.rs`): the update call of the shared model with 0, 4 and 16 readers. The update's 99th percentile rises from
  5 µs to 29 µs at 16 readers.
