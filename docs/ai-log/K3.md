## Summary

- K1 (`control/machine.rs`, `control/mod.rs`): `Event::Deferred { outcome }`; a positive outcome completes an enhanced command waiting for the change; a
  negative one terminates it with its cause and ends the selection. D-027.
- Async (`control.rs`): the outcomes are reported through `ControlHandle::complete`, queued, and applied at the next poll; an unknown reference is refused.
- Async (`client_control.rs`): `select`, `select_with_value`, `operate`, `cancel` on `IecClient`; `termination` decodes the CommandTermination and the
  LastApplError; `cause_name` names an additional cause.
- The checks stay synchronous: the MMS write answers at once. A deferred response of a write needs a change of the MMS layer (recorded in D-027).
