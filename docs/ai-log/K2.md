## Summary

- `igs-iec61850-mms/src/control.rs`: the MMS mapping on the served model (D-026). Reads of `SBO` are selects; writes of `SBOw`, `Oper` and `Cancel`
  are the other services; negative answers and failed commands send `LastApplError`; the termination sends `CommandTermination`. The checks and the
  execution go through `ControlHandler`.
- `igs-iec61850-mms/src/model.rs`: the dispatch separates the control items of a read or a write from the others, which the VMD keeps; the control
  objects are built from the configuration (`ctlModel`, the times, the behaviour); the poll and the release follow the machines.
- S3 (`scl/build.rs`) keeps the initial values of the basic data attributes (a `Val` directly under its data object), applied to the served model at
  their MMS type. This closes the gap of D-022 for `ctlModel`.
- K1 (`control/machine.rs`): a normal-security selection carries no origin, test flag or value; its sequence is fixed by the first operation (D-026).
  The K1 test of the sequence is rewritten on the enhanced model, and a test of the normal selection is added.
- The two gaps of the text are recorded: table 100 is cut in the prepared text (the mapping of the other refusals is provisional), and the
  `LastApplError` follows the write response (the standard puts it before).
